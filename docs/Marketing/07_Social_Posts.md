# Social Posts — Draft Bank (Edition A, the demo)

> Ready-to-post copy for the channels in
> [02_Media_Marketing_Plan.md](02_Media_Marketing_Plan.md), each paired with
> the **imagery it needs** — so this file doubles as the shot list for
> trailer and screenshot capture.
>
> **Scope: edition A only** (docs/Design/Alpha_Build.md §4): Age 0, the
> Alanthor culture, and the four sects of the **House of Vaelith** —
> Antiquity, Renewal, Fortitude, Prosperity. Every post and every shot must
> be capturable in an A build. No other culture, no other sect, and no
> "more cultures coming" teases.
>
> **Written 2026-10-19 against the current design** (docs/Design/), not the
> May pitch in 01: there is no Glow, no "Border" PvE layer, no Hall. If a
> post here and a Design doc disagree, the Design doc wins and the post is
> rewritten. No stat numbers in public copy; they change with every balance
> pass.

### Capture conventions for every image below

- **Dusk grade, fog of war ON** unless the note says otherwise — the navy
  mist is part of the look (Art_Direction.md).
- **HUD hidden** unless the note asks for UI. There is no clean-screen
  toggle yet; until there is, crop, or frame the UI out from a replay.
- **Film from replays.** Play the match once, then capture the moment as
  often as needed from different angles (pause / speed / timeline scrub).
- Lean on **emissives**: lantern windows, cyan veilstone, purple curse.
  Dark is not mood — every shot needs a light source in it.

## 3. Territory explainer

**Channels:** Reddit (r/RealTimeStrategy), devlog cross-post

> **Title:** In our RTS, land is only yours while your army stands on it
>
> There's no territory cap. You hold as much ground as you can defend.
>
> Each territory has an ownership meter. Your soldiers standing in it fill
> it, weighted by how much of an army they are. If enemies are in there too,
> it freezes. If you leave it empty and unbuilt, it decays.
>
> Buildings hold ground. Fortresses and extractors lock it. If you lose the
> territory, everything you built on it comes down with it, and so does any
> ground cut off from your Fortress network.
>
> The result: borders actually move, and a raid on a back territory can
> really hurt.
>
> Happy to answer anything about how it works. (GIF below.)

**Imagery:** A 10-15 s GIF. Camera fixed above one territory border. A
squad walks in and the ownership meter fills, the border line slides over
the territory, and a foundation appears. Then an enemy squad arrives, the
meter freezes and the fight starts. **Show the meter on screen** — this is
the one shot where UI belongs.

### C7. Reddit long-form: "We gave our RTS a One Ring"

## 16. Dev-to-dev: "why there's only two ages"

**Channels:** Bluesky thread, devlog

> **1/** Most RTS games have four or five ages. We have two. Here's why. 🧵
>
> **2/** Age 0 is a mirror match. Same buildings, same units, same
> options. The skill is reading the map and your opponent.
>
> **3/** After age-up the depth is sideways, not upward: building levels
> that apply to every building of that type at once, sects and their
> powers, heroes who level only through combat.
>
> **4/** That keeps the late game about decisions, not about getting
> through a tier list.

**Imagery:** (1) Split screen of one start location, Age 0 on the left and
Age 1 on the right. (2) Two identical starts mirrored. (3) The religion quick
menu with power cells. UI is the subject here, so capture it clean at 1080p
or higher. (4) A late-game battle wide shot with every light source on.
